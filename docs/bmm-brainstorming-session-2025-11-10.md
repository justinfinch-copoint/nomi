# Brainstorming Session Results

**Session Date:** 2025-11-10
**Facilitator:** Business Analyst Mary
**Participant:** BMad

## Executive Summary

**Topic:** Feature ideas and capabilities for Nomi - a reimagined todo list application

**Session Goals:** Explore features that bridge daily tasks with inspiration capture, and help people turn ideas into reality without getting overwhelmed

**Techniques Used:** What If Scenarios (creative), Forced Relationships (creative), SCAMPER Method (structured)

**Total Ideas Generated:** 38 distinct feature concepts

### Key Themes Identified:

1. **From Tracking to Orchestration** - Nomi doesn't just track what you need to do, it actively helps you get outcomes achieved through the most efficient means
2. **Human-Centered Intelligence** - Mood, energy, complexity, and time of day all matter. Nomi works WITH your natural rhythms, not against them
3. **Effort Elimination** - Remove all manual busy work (categorizing, prioritizing, organizing) so you can focus on actual doing
4. **Outcome Over Process** - It's not about checking boxes, it's about achieving results and building verified skills

## Technique Sessions

### Technique #1: What If Scenarios (15 minutes)

**Purpose:** Explore radical possibilities by removing all constraints

**Key Ideas Generated:**
- Voice-activated personal assistant ("Hey Nomi, remind me to..." / "Hey Nomi, I just had an idea to...")
- Always-available capture, especially during walks and moments of inspiration
- Intelligent scheduling by task complexity (complex → morning fresh brain time, simple → later in day)
- Focus days (one big complex task) vs Productivity days (many small tasks)
- AI-powered idea research and automatic evaluation
- Automatic filtering of weak ideas to focus on the best
- Idea expansion - good ideas generate more inspiration
- Personal energy pattern learning (e.g., "BMad has best ideas during walks")
- Contextual questioning to capture full inspiration context in the moment

**Breakthrough Moment:** Recognition that task scheduling should be based on cognitive load and energy, not arbitrary due dates

### Technique #2: Forced Relationships (15 minutes)

**Purpose:** Connect unrelated concepts to spark innovative features

**Pairing #1: Todo Lists + Music Playlist**
- Mood-based task curation ("I'm feeling inspired" → creative tasks, "I'm tired" → mindless/outdoor tasks)
- Task Mixes: "Morning Momentum Mix", "Wind Down Mix", "Fresh Air Mix", "Deep Focus Mix"
- Shuffle feature - when you can't decide, Nomi picks something appropriate for your current state

**Pairing #2: Inspiration Capture + Garden/Gardening**
- AI-Generated Inspiration Seeds - proactive inspiration delivery based on your interests/goals
- Instant seed-to-todo conversion
- Idea Greenhouse - protected space for half-formed inspirations to grow without pressure
- Compost Pile - save failed ideas because pieces might be useful later
- Nurturing ideas over time (not every seed needs to sprout immediately)

**Breakthrough Moment:** Realizing Nomi could proactively generate personalized inspiration, not just capture it

### Technique #3: SCAMPER Method (20 minutes)

**Purpose:** Systematically examine features through seven transformation lenses

**S - Substitute:**
- Replace manual text entry with forwarding (calendar invites, text messages, emails)
- Auto-extract action items from digital life
- Screenshot capture → task creation

**C - Combine:**
- Task management + Outsourcing/delegation (TaskRabbit, Fiverr integration)
- Community crowdsourcing and skill trades
- One-tap delegation - shift from "tracking tasks" to "getting things done"

**A - Adapt:**
- Gaming XP system → Professional skill portfolio
- Earn XP from completing tasks and pushing forward ideas
- Shareable living resume showing verified capabilities (proof of doing, not just claiming)
- Employers could see verified skill levels

**M - Modify:**
- Fundamentally change from deadline-driven to efficiency-driven scheduling
- AI determines task type and optimal scheduling
- Days have personalities optimized for YOUR efficiency
- Remove guilt/stress of due dates, replace with intelligent work optimization

**P - Put to Other Uses:**
- Mood selector → mental health tracking
- Skill XP system → hiring/recruiting tool
- AI research feature → learning tool
- Completed inspiration research → portfolio evidence of innovation capability

**E - Eliminate:**
- No manual priorities (AI knows what matters)
- No categories (AI organizes automatically)
- No recurring task setup complexity
- No manual due dates (unless specifically needed)
- Eliminate all friction and busywork

**R - Reverse:**
- From "You ask what to do" → "Nomi proactively suggests perfect tasks for your state"
- From "Tasks disappear when complete" → "Completions become MORE visible (XP, portfolio)"
- From "Inspiration sits there" → "Nomi IMMEDIATELY acts on it (researches, evaluates, proposes)"
- From "My todo list = things I must do myself" → "My todo list = outcomes to achieve by most efficient means"

**Breakthrough Moment:** The reversal that not all tasks need to be done by you personally - optimize for outcome achievement, not task completion

## Idea Categorization

### Immediate Opportunities

_MVP / Foundation - Ideas ready to implement now_

**Core Differentiators (Must-Have for Launch):**

1. **Efficiency-driven scheduling** (not deadline-driven) - Schedule tasks based on when they'll be done best, not arbitrary due dates

### Insights and Learnings

_Key realizations from the session_

**Core Insight:**
"Productivity isn't about priority lists - it's about matching the right task to the right moment based on how you feel and when you're most effective."

Traditional todo apps assume you're a constant productivity machine. But humans have:
- High energy mornings vs low energy afternoons
- Inspired days vs tired days
- Focus capacity vs multitask capacity
- Creative moments vs execution moments

**Nomi respects and optimizes for this reality.** That's the fundamental innovation.

**Other Key Learnings:**
- Not everything needs to be done by you personally (delegate/outsource for efficiency)
- Inspiration and tasks aren't separate - they're a continuum that needs bridging
- Proof of work (XP/skills) is more valuable than just task completion
- Friction kills productivity - remove ALL unnecessary manual work
- Getting stuff done is tied to how you feel and when you should do things to be most productive

## Action Planning

### Top 3 Priority Ideas

These three priorities form the complete foundation for Nomi: capture both tasks AND ideas → schedule intelligently → show meaningful progress.

#### #1 Priority: Smart Task Management Foundation

**What it includes:**
- Task capture (manual input to start - keep it simple)
- AI-driven complexity analysis (automatically classify tasks)
- AI-driven intelligent scheduling (based on complexity, mood, energy)
- NO due dates (efficiency-driven, not deadline-driven)

**Rationale:** This proves the core hypothesis - that scheduling based on task complexity + human state is better than traditional deadline management. This is Nomi's fundamental differentiator.

**Next steps:**
- Define task complexity scoring system (simple, moderate, complex, creative)
- Design mood/energy selector interface (keep lightweight, not burdensome)
- Build AI task classification model
- Create intelligent scheduling algorithm
- Design daily view that respects mood/energy/time

**Resources needed:**
- AI/ML expertise for task classification
- UX designer for mood selector interface
- Backend engineer for scheduling algorithm
- Database schema for tasks + metadata

**Timeline:** MVP Phase - Core foundation (Months 1-3)

#### #2 Priority: Inspiration-to-Action Pipeline

**What it includes:**
- Separate capture mode for inspiration vs tasks
- System to evaluate/prioritize ideas (not all ideas are worth pursuing)
- Bridge from inspiration → actionable tasks
- Lightweight idea management (don't over-complicate)

**Rationale:** This is the other half of Nomi's soul - preventing overwhelm by helping users decide which ideas to pursue and naturally converting worthy inspirations into scheduled work. This is what makes Nomi more than just a smart todo app.

**Next steps:**
- Design inspiration capture interface (differentiate from task capture)
- Define criteria for idea evaluation (AI-assisted filtering)
- Create inspiration-to-task workflow
- Build idea storage/review system
- Design "idea garden" visualization

**Resources needed:**
- AI model for idea evaluation/scoring
- Database schema for ideas vs tasks (with relationships)
- UI/UX design for dual-mode capture
- Research on idea prioritization frameworks

**Timeline:** MVP Phase - Secondary priority (Months 2-4)

#### #3 Priority: Progress Visibility & Skill Building

**What it includes:**
- Visible record of what you've accomplished (completions don't disappear)
- Skill-based XP earned from task completion
- Progress visualization (you're doing more than you realize)

**Rationale:** People abandon todo apps because they feel like endless treadmills. Nomi shows "look how much you've accomplished" and "look at the skills you're building" - that's powerful motivation and retention.

**Next steps:**
- Design XP/skill taxonomy (what skills map to what tasks?)
- Create completion history visualization
- Build skill progression tracking system
- Design motivational feedback (balance motivation without being cheesy)
- Define shareable profile format for future

**Resources needed:**
- Task-to-skill mapping logic and taxonomy
- Progress dashboard UI design
- Data persistence for historical completions
- Gamification design expertise

**Timeline:** MVP Phase - Can start simple (basic completion log + XP) and evolve (Months 2-5)

## Reflection and Follow-up

### What Worked Well

**Most Productive Techniques:**
- **Forced Relationships** (playlist metaphor, garden metaphor) sparked some of the most innovative features - mood-based curation, AI-generated inspiration seeds, and the entire "idea garden" concept
- **SCAMPER's Reverse lens** helped crystallize the fundamental "get it done vs do it yourself" philosophy and the shift from task completion to outcome achievement
- **What If Scenarios** established the bold vision without constraints, setting the creative tone for the session

**Session Flow:**
The progression from creative/exploratory techniques (What If, Forced Relationships) to structured refinement (SCAMPER) worked well. Started broad, then systematically examined features from multiple angles.

**Energy & Momentum:**
Strong momentum throughout. Participant engaged deeply with each technique and built ideas progressively rather than starting from scratch each time.

### Areas for Further Exploration

**Critical Questions Requiring Deeper Investigation:**

1. **AI Complexity Scoring** - How does Nomi determine if a task is "complex creative work" vs "simple mindless task"? What signals/keywords/patterns indicate cognitive load?

2. **Mood/Energy Interface** - How do users tell Nomi how they're feeling without it being burdensome? Needs to be lightweight (not detailed emotional check-ins)

3. **Idea Evaluation Criteria** - What makes an idea "worth pursuing"? How does AI assess idea quality/feasibility/alignment with user goals?

4. **XP-to-Skill Mapping** - How do tasks map to professional skills? What taxonomy makes sense? How granular should skills be?

5. **Privacy & Trust** - Nomi knows a lot about you (energy patterns, ideas, work habits, mood states). How to handle privacy considerations and build user trust?

6. **Deadline Exceptions** - How to handle tasks that DO have actual deadlines (taxes, appointments, event RSVPs)?

7. **Mismatched States** - What happens when someone feels inspired but only has mindless tasks available? Or feels tired but has urgent complex work?

### Recommended Follow-up Techniques

For deeper exploration of the critical questions above:

- **First Principles Thinking** - Strip away assumptions to answer "What fundamentally makes a task complex?" Rebuild from core truths
- **Five Whys** - Understand user motivation around mood/energy tracking and when/why they'd actually use it
- **Morphological Analysis** - Systematically explore all possible combinations of task properties, mood states, and scheduling options
- **Question Storming** - Properly define the problem space before building solutions (especially for AI complexity scoring)
- **User Journey Mapping** - Map how users would actually interact with Nomi throughout a typical day/week

### Questions That Emerged

1. **Balance Intelligence with Intrusiveness:** How does Nomi balance being intelligent/proactive without being intrusive or creepy?

2. **Minimum Viable Mood Selector:** What's the simplest way to capture user state without creating burden?

3. **Handling Real Deadlines:** How to integrate tasks that DO have actual deadlines into an efficiency-driven system?

4. **Mismatched Expectations:** What happens when user state doesn't match available tasks?

5. **Privacy & Data:** What are the privacy implications of Nomi learning your patterns, and how to build trust?

6. **Delegation Decision Logic:** How does Nomi recommend "do vs delegate" without being pushy?

7. **Skill Taxonomy:** What skill categories make sense that are both meaningful and not overwhelming?
